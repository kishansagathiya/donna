1. ONE-LINER (top of memo)
   → What you do + for whom + why it's different in 10-15 words
   → Use contrarian "no X, no Y" or "X for Y" format

2. TEAM (immediately after)
   → Why YOUR experience = exact founder-market fit for THIS problem
   → Context box if prior company isn't universally known
   → Frame as "I lived this problem at scale" not just "I worked at X"

3. PROBLEM (the crescendo)
   → Hook: bold contrarian statement
   → Credible stat from respected source
   → Relatable pain (demo vs production gap)
   → 4-5 bullet failure modes
   → One concrete horror story (real, ideally viral)
   → Math closer showing the problem is systemic

4. WHY CURRENT FIXES FAIL ← NEW: the vacuum-creation section
   → "So how are people fixing this today? Spoiler: badly."
   → 3-4 attempted approaches, each with specific failure mode
   → Use the "menu of misery" framing — make each option feel exhausting
   → Highlighted punch line: "Nobody is fixing [specific gap]"
   → Bridge: "That's precisely the gap [your product] fills."

5. SOLUTION (concrete + numbered)
   → Verb-based numbered process (Watch → Diagnose → Fix → Validate → Inject)
   → Each step is one sentence, plain English
   → Call out your key differentiator TWICE (like Bento's "readable text files")
   → Simple metaphor to anchor ("like giving your agent a brain that remembers")

6. PROOF ← NEW: the "show me the numbers" section
   → One hard benchmark with specific model + specific numbers
   → The comparison should be shocking: cheap model + your tool > expensive model alone
   → Include cost per run (makes it feel real, not theoretical)
   → "Let that sink in" moment

7. WHO BUYS + WHY THEY PAY (buyer table)
   → 3-4 buyer segments with specific examples
   → Each row answers: "why will they pay?" and "why can't they build this themselves?"

8. COMPETITION (comparison table)
   → Format: Solution | What it does | What it misses
   → Your product is the last row with "None of the above limitations"
   → Disarming intro line

9. MOAT (compounding argument)
   → Why this gets stronger over time
   → Why competitors can copy the tech but not accumulated value
   → Anchor to a known comparable ("Like Datadog, but for X")

10. RISKS (honest + hedged)
    → Name the biggest risk
    → Provide a STRUCTURAL hedge

11. WHY YOU / WHY NOW
    → Market timing + your specific unfair advantage
    → Scarcity line if pitching a specific fund

12. THE DEAL
    → Clean table, standard terms, boring
