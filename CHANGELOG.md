# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Personal Assistant (2026-08-26)

Donna v1 is the personal assistant release: a voice-first product on web and iOS that remembers you, keeps a working memory from notes and chats, and runs tasks on cloud while your device is locked.

### In this release

- **Chat & voice** — text chat, dictation, and realtime Voice on web and iOS
- **Memory & notes** — notes, tags, search, Today briefing, and a structured memory of people, projects, and preferences
- **Actions** — calendar, email, drafts, and other integrations with an approve-before-irreversible inbox
- **Cloud agents** — multi-step runs with live steps, cancel/redirect, skills, subagents, and an interactive browser
- **AI employees** — durable goals that keep taking shifts in the background
- **Schedules & reminders** — recurring cloud goals and timed alerts
- **Clients** — web app and iOS (App Store)

The iOS App Store marketing version remains independent of this product generation (`1.0.x` builds).
