So how are people fixing this today? Spoiler: badly.

| Attempt | What you get | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT / Claude Chat** | Answers and some task execution | They surely do some tasks like researching, but primarily that just do Question Answer |

| **Cowork (Codex Work, Claude Cowork)** | Coding Harnesses kinda re-badged for everyone else | Haven't got much love, need to keep the laptop on|


**Nobody is fixing both halves of a personal assistant at once: memory, and infrastructure that turns that context into completed work.**

That is precisely the gap Donna fills.

## 5. SOLUTION

Donna is a **voice-first AI personal assistant** on **iOS and web**.

It turns conversations and notes into **memory, plans, and tasks** — then uses **AI agents** that run on cloud to complete the tasks software can execute.

Like having an assistant who was in the room, remembers your people and projects, and then does the busywork.

### How it works

1. **Capture** — Speak or type into the iOS app or website; notes and voice become your working context.
2. **Remember** — Conversations become structured understanding of people, projects, priorities, responsibilities.
3. **Plan** — Notes convert into actions and plans.
4. **Act** — Agents complete digital tasks (today: **schedule calendar meetings**; more integrations next).
5. **Talk** — Voice/chat harness for fast, everyday conversation.

**Core insight (said twice on purpose):** Memory without action becomes another archive. Action without memory is just another command interface. A real personal assistant needs **both** — continuous context and the infrastructure to turn that context into completed work.

**Still building:** cloud agents created from notes; memory quality at larger note volume; more integrations beyond calendar.
