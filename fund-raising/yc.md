# Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don’t?

Closest competitors are Omi, Amazon Bee, HeyPocket and Plaud on dedicated hardware that turns conversations into notes, memories, and tasks, and ChatGPT and other AI agents on executing digital tasks on cloud.

We think these companies are each building one half of the personal assistant. The memory products mostly stop after telling you what happened or what you should do. The agent products can perform tasks, but usually require you to explicitly instruct them and provide the relevant context each time.

Donna combines both. Its dedicated voice device makes it frictionless to capture thoughts, conversations, commitments, and decisions as they happen. Donna builds a persistent, structured understanding of the user’s people, projects, priorities, and responsibilities. It then identifies what needs to be done and independently completes the tasks that software can execute.

Our core insight is that memory without action becomes another archive, while action without memory is just another command interface. A real personal assistant needs both: continuous context and the infrastructure to turn that context into completed work.
